## Things I've built

### Hackathons and side projects

**[ShipDoc](https://shipdoc.org)** · Averis × Monash Hackathon 2026<br>
Reads a shipping-documentation inbox, sorts every email and checks each draft Bill of Lading against its Shipping Instruction, handing anything it can't decide to a person with the reason. Getting it onto AWS was my job, and when the hackathon's AWS account expired I moved it to my homelab.<br>
`Python` `AWS Lambda` `DynamoDB` `Docker` · [code](https://github.com/VRP2206/Averis_hackathon_2026) · [docs](https://docs.shipdoc.org) · [write-up](https://blog.vrp2206.com/posts/shipdoc-aws/)

**Anxin (安心)** · MUBA Blockchain Hackathon 2026 · team lead<br>
Paste a message or a link, or upload a screenshot, and get a scam and misinformation report in English and Simplified Chinese, with every AI judgement made on Gonka's decentralised inference network. It made the top 21 of its track.<br>
`Python` `Docker` `Tesseract OCR` · [code](https://github.com/VRP2206/MUBA_Hackathon_2026) · [write-up](https://blog.vrp2206.com/posts/anxin-muba-hackathon/)

**[Cadence](https://cadence-blond-two.vercel.app/)** · Shortcut Asia Internship Challenge<br>
A renewal tracker for road tax, licences, passports, insurance and subscriptions that explains *when* and *why* something needs attention instead of sending a wall of alerts.<br>
`TypeScript` `React` `Vite` `Vitest` · [code](https://github.com/VRP2206/cadence)

**ExamX** · built for my FIT2014 exam<br>
Turns past papers into new practice papers with worked solutions and marks your attempts, on a local model or your own Claude or Gemini key. Paused for now so I can actually study.<br>
`Python` `PyMuPDF` `Ollama` `Docker` · [code](https://github.com/VRP2206/Exam_X) · [write-up](https://blog.vrp2206.com/posts/examx-part-1/)

### From my coursework

**[Pathfinding Visualizer](https://pathfinding.vrp2206.com)** · FIT1045 HD project<br>
Compare A\*, Dijkstra and RRT on the same grid: set the obstacle density, place a start and a goal, watch each algorithm explore, then compare runtime, nodes expanded and path length side by side.<br>
`Python` `Streamlit` `Plotly` `NumPy` · [code](https://github.com/VRP2206/Pathfinding_Visualizer) · [revisited](https://blog.vrp2206.com/posts/pathfinding-revisited/)

**[Roguelike Adventure](https://roguelike.vrp2206.com)** · FIT1045<br>
A data-driven dungeon crawler where classes, allies, perks, shops, room types and boss behaviour live in JSON, so a new enemy is an edit to a file rather than a new branch in a function.<br>
`Python` `Streamlit` `JSON` · [code](https://github.com/VRP2206/Roguelike_Game)

**[FairSight](https://prototype.fairsight.org)** · FIT1055 group project<br>
A platform that audits AI hiring systems for bias and explains every decision. I built the Streamlit prototype, then [reran it months later](https://blog.vrp2206.com/posts/fairsight-revisited/) and found the bias in my bias detector was twice what I'd claimed.<br>
`Python` `Streamlit` · [code](https://github.com/VRP2206/fairsight_prototype) · [project site](https://fairsight.org)

Smaller tools live in [my repositories](https://github.com/VRP2206?tab=repositories), like an Ed Discussion exporter that saves a course's threads and lessons as JSON and Markdown.
